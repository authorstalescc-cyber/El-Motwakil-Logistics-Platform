Privacy Policy

1. Data Protection Commitment

Al-Mutawakkil Transport Contracting values client privacy and security. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through our official website is handled.

2. Information Collection & Use

We collect information provided voluntarily through our quotation form (such as your name, company name, phone number, and shipment specifications). This data is strictly used to evaluate your transport requirements and prepare accurate price quotations.

3. No Server Storage of Quotation Forms

Quotation requests submitted via our website form generate an encrypted direct communication message sent via WhatsApp directly to our dispatch desk. We do not store or sell customer data to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or aggressive third-party advertising monitors. We utilize standard web browser session mechanisms necessary for optimal site performance and language preferences.

5. External Links

Our website may contain external links to Google Maps or third-party logistics platforms. We recommend reviewing the privacy terms of any third-party services accessed through external links.

6. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions regarding our privacy policy or data handling, please contact us via email at ahmed@almutawakkil.com.
