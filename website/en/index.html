What We Move

Six specialized transport services covering containerized, reefer, LCL, general, heavy equipment, and bulk liquid cargo.

Container Transport — Import & Export

20ft, 40ft & 45ft containers from/to Alexandria, El-Dekheila, and Abu Qir ports.

Reefer Containers

40ft reefer units with active genset power supply, maintaining cold chain integrity throughout transit.

LCL Consolidated Cargo

Jumbo 4-meter & Pickup trucks — enclosed or open-flatbed based on cargo nature.

General & Bulk Cargo

Raw materials, factory production lines, and bulk shipments using tailored transport equipment.

Heavy Equipment & Oversized Cargo

Lowbed trailers with full lashing, route permits, and escort arrangements when needed.

Flexitank Services

Bulk liquid transportation inside 20ft containers using specialized flexitank fitting.
