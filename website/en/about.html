01 / 06 ABOUT US

Company Overview

Al-Mutawakkil Transport Contracting is a land transport company headquartered in El-Mansheya, Alexandria. Established officially in 2021, its foundation rests on over 10 years of prior executive port operations management inside Alexandrian commercial ports.

We transport from Alexandria, El-Dekheila, and Abu Qir ports, as well as associated customs zones, to all governorates across Egypt. Our experienced team manages every shipment from berth loading to safe, timely site delivery.

We operate licensed fleet units driven by seasoned drivers. The primary operational difference with Al-Mutawakkil is that management supervises operations directly — on-site representatives manage dockside procedures, and replacement units are dispatched immediately if needed without delay.

02 / 06 OUR TIMELINE

Our Journey

From hands-on port berth management to executing daily dispatches across all trailer categories.

  1. Genesis

    Field Experience inside Alexandria Ports

    Before adopting the corporate identity, management operated for over a decade in container and freight handling across Alexandria ports — building practical knowledge of customs procedures, cargo handling requirements, and delay risk mitigation.

  2. 2021

    Official Establishment

    Al-Mutawakkil officially launched land transport operations, focusing initially on import and export container movements connecting Alexandrian ports to industrial hubs in the Delta and Greater Cairo.

  3. Expansion

    Dedicated Equipment for Every Cargo Type

    Expanded services to encompass bulk raw materials, industrial machinery, temperature-sensitive reefers, LCL consolidated shipments, and oversized heavy equipment transported on specialized lowbed trailers.

  4. Capability

    Excellence in Complex Shipments

    Proven execution in mission-critical logistics: oversized agricultural harvesters, complete factory production lines, and temperature-strict reefer containers — setting the standard for reliable transport.

  5. Today

    Daily Dispatches & Nationwide Coverage

    Serving major international shipping lines, customs brokers, and industrial manufacturers with daily dispatches connecting commercial ports to all 27 Egyptian governorates.
