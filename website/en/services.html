Our Transport Services

Click on any service to view operational specifications, equipment details, and handling procedures.

01. Container Transport — Import & Export

Flatbed and container chassis trailers equipped to handle 20ft, 40ft, and 45ft containers (Standard & High Cube), as well as Open Top and Flat Rack units.

  • Daily dispatches from Alexandria, El-Dekheila, and Abu Qir ports.
  • Single-20ft, double-20ft, and heavy 40ft container chassis.
  • Port dockside representation for gate scale & inspection.

02. Reefer Container Transport

40ft reefer containers for temperature-sensitive foodstuffs, pharmaceuticals, and agricultural exports requiring strict cold chain maintenance.

  • Chassis mounted clip-on Genset generators operating 24/7.
  • Continuous temperature logging and monitoring throughout transit.
  • Direct dispatch from reefer terminal plugs without delay.

03. LCL Consolidated Cargo

Partial shipments and consolidated cargo cleared from customs warehouses, transported via dedicated Jumbo 4m and Pickup trucks.

  • Jumbo 4-meter trucks (enclosed box or open tarpaulin).
  • Pickup trucks for small urgent dispatches and spare parts.
  • Direct warehouse loading and door-to-door delivery.

04. General & Bulk Cargo

Raw material bags, timber, steel coils, factory raw goods, and bulk shipments transported via heavy-duty flatbeds and side-wall trailers.

  • 12m to 13.6m flatbed trailers with stanchions & sideboards.
  • Heavy-duty lashing straps, chains, and waterproof tarpaulins.
  • Direct factory production line transport schedules.

05. Oversized & Heavy Equipment

Specialized lowbed trailers for construction machinery, excavators, harvesters, transformers, and out-of-gauge (OOG) project cargo.

  • Multi-axle lowbed trailers for high payload capacity.
  • Route survey, bridge clearance checks, and traffic permits.
  • Heavy-duty chain lashing and specialized escort vehicles.

06. Flexitank Liquid Transport

Transportation of non-hazardous bulk liquids (vegetable oils, food grade liquids, industrial fluids) installed inside 20ft dry containers.

  • Container inspection & bulkhead installation verification.
  • Smooth shock-free transport preserving flexitank integrity.
  • Port export berth dispatch & factory receiving protocols.
